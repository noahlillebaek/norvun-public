Architecture & Research

← Norvun overview

Norvun's intelligence ecosystem is designed around three complementary perspectives.

Pillar Research question
Financial Intelligence What does a company's financial reporting establish?
Economic Intelligence How is an industry or company connected to the real economy?
Capital Flow Intelligence What can dated ownership and capital-position evidence establish?

Norvun Core is the intended integration and synthesis layer, bringing company-level evidence, economic context and capital-market observations into a connected intelligence ecosystem.

Engineering principles

  • Evidence and provenance before confident conclusions.
  • Repeatable calculations and verification where feasible.
  • Explicit separation of observations, estimates and hypothetical scenarios.
  • Refusal to infer unsupported results.

Norvun Capital explores the application of this intelligence to investment research. Implementation details and investment strategies remain private.
